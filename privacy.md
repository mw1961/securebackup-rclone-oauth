# Privacy Policy — SecureBackup-Rclone

Last updated: September 10, 2026

SecureBackup-Rclone is a personal, non-commercial application used solely by its owner for encrypted backup storage on Google Drive.

## Google Drive Access

The application uses Rclone to access Google Drive. Access is limited to the permissions granted through Google OAuth.

## Data Handling

Backup data is encrypted locally by Restic before being transferred to Google Drive.

SecureBackup-Rclone does not sell, share, monetize, or disclose user data to third parties.

The application is not offered as a service to other users.

## Data Storage

Encrypted backup data is stored in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials and backup encryption credentials are maintained by the application owner and are not intentionally transmitted to third parties other than as required for authentication with Google.

## Contact

This application is operated solely for personal use.
